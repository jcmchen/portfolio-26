In a world where resources were scarce and the population had grown beyond what the planet could sustain, robots were created to help humans in their daily lives. Amongst them emerges two parties, sharing one common goal: to secure resources for their respective teams. As the news of the discovery of a new energy source brings into the open, a battle between the two teams is destined to ensue.

The battle takes part in a field including the base of both teams (red and blue), with robots racing against time, gathering scattered materials. Protector robots guard the bases, blocking any uninvited guests that try to enter. Amongst them, a third party rises out of the blue…
