This project explore Collective Robotic Construction (CRC), specifically how agent-based modelling and simulation (AMBS) could be utilized for the design and control of automated construction.

<em>
  In a world where resources were scarce and the population had grown beyond
  what the planet could sustain, robots were created to help humans in their
  daily lives. Amongst them emerges two parties, sharing one common goal: to
  secure resources for their respective teams. As the news of the discovery of a
  new energy source brings into the open, a battle between the two teams is
  destined to ensue.{" "}
</em>

<em>
  The battle takes part in a field including the base of both teams (red and
  blue), with robots racing against time, gathering scattered materials.
  Protector robots guard the bases, blocking any uninvited guests that try to
  enter. Amongst them, a third party rises out of the blue…{" "}
</em>
