<div className="row h-4" />
In this one-week project, users can create vibrant digital art by pressing and
twisting (analog inputs). Users can generate new branches by mouse clicks on the
canvas. When branches stop growing, users can also press the Force Sensitive
Resistor to generate new branches from the end of the last branches. The colors
of branches could be manipulated by two potentiometers that control G (green)
and B (blue) in the RGB system. Actions could be done simultaneously in this
work.
